# Executive memo — Rewind Expansion Intelligence
**To:** Revenue Operations leadership · **From:** Felipe Mattos · **Status:** Independent proposal; no affiliation with Rewind

## Decision to explore
Rewind's SaaS data protection portfolio creates an interesting Revenue Operations question: can account teams recognize where broader coverage *might* be relevant without mistaking platform usage for purchase intent?

## Proposed operating principle
**Qualify the conversation before creating the opportunity.** Bring together the fictional customer's protected products, observed application inventory, recency, recovery importance, account-engagement permissions and planned customer discussions. Use transparent rules to suggest the next human review step, not a deal stage or forecast.

## Three examples from the fictional fixture
1. **Northstar Commerce:** recent inventory, critical recovery need and near-term discussion. Proposed owner: Customer Success + Account Manager. Ask whether GitHub needs independent recovery coverage.
2. **Meridian Systems:** high-priority need, but the inventory is 180 days old. Verify the application inventory before any expansion conversation.
3. **Atlas Retail Group:** potential coverage signal, but outreach permission is not confirmed. Consult the account owner first; do not turn this into unsolicited outreach.

## Proposed pilot
- **Discover:** interview account owners about existing signals and how they define appropriate expansion conversations.
- **Validate:** use permissioned sources and agree on freshness, recovery criticality and contact requirements.
- **Shadow-run:** present a small set of suggested conversations to sellers and CSMs without writing to production CRM.
- **Evaluate:** track false positives, whether advice was actionable, and whether it helped focus the team's time. Do not attribute changes in ARR without an approved measurement design.
