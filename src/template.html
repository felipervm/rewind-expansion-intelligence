03 / Proposed pilot for Revenue Operations

Start by learning. Then test.

  1. Align: ask account teams which coverage and recovery signals genuinely help conversations.
  2. Validate: reconcile product coverage and inventory recency with approved sources.
  3. Shadow review: test a handful of suggested actions with account owners before putting anything into CRM.
  4. Learn: capture objections, false positives and customer questions to improve the process.
